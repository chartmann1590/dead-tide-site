Privacy Policy — Dead Tide: Zombie Survivor

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Dead Tide: Zombie Survivor (package com.deadtide.hordesurvivor) is developed by Hartmann Studios. This policy explains what data the app itself handles and what data is handled by the advertising service built into the app.

Data the app itself collects

The game itself collects no personal data and no usage analytics, has no account system, and runs no analytics or crash-reporting service. Game progress — in-game gold, talent upgrades, best results, settings, and one saved run — is stored only on your device in the app's private storage. It never leaves your device, is never sent to us, and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

Advertising — Google AdMob

Dead Tide contains ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a banner on the menu, an optional rewarded ad, and an interstitial shown after a run ends — never during gameplay. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and share data including:

  • Device / advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic / performance data about the ads SDK.

Google processes this data as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure (https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy). Where required (EEA/UK), the app presents Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before ads are requested, and ads are only requested when the gathered consent state allows it. You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your device Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Data we share

Other than the AdMob data described above, we share nothing: we collect nothing ourselves, so we sell nothing and transfer nothing to any other third party.

Children

Dead Tide is not directed at children. It is a zombie-themed action game with cartoon/fantasy violence and blood effects, intended for a general adult and teen audience, and it is not part of the Google Play Families program.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the app's Google Play Data Safety section will be updated to match before that version is released.
