DEAD TIDE

Survive the endless undead horde.

A top-down zombie horde survivor for Android. Auto-fire weapons, one-thumb movement, level-ups every minute and a squad to rescue. How long can you hold the line?

Coming soon to Google Play

Built for quick, brutal runs

Every run is a few frantic minutes. Every run makes you stronger.

🧟An endless undead tide

Waves of shamblers, brutes and abominations pour in from every side. Your guns fire on their own: you move, dash and survive.

⬆️Level up mid-run

Every level offers three upgrades. Stack damage, speed and new weapons into a build that melts the horde.

🚀A real arsenal

Rocket Launcher, Combat Shotgun, Rail Sniper, Plasma Bolt, Storm Drone, Frost Nova, Chain Lightning, Flame Aura, Starfall and more.

🪖Rescue your squad

Find Sarge, Ghost and Sparks by their beacons. Each survivor you pull out joins the fight.

💀Chapters and bosses

Fight from the Cursed Graveyard to the Crimson Catacombs, the Frozen Desolation and Toxic Quarantine, each with its own boss.

🛡️Permanent progress

Spend the gold you salvage on armory talents between runs: more damage, more health, faster boots, a better magnet.

FAQ

Is Dead Tide free?

Yes. Dead Tide is free to play with optional ads. You can remove ads or buy gold packs with a one-time in-app purchase, but you never need to pay to finish the campaign.

When does it come out?

Dead Tide is coming soon to Google Play for Android phones. This page will link straight to the store the moment it is live.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account. Your progress is saved on your phone. Ads need a connection; the game itself plays fine without one.

Where do I report a bug?

Open an issue on GitHub and include your phone model.
